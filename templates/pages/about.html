From group to center

The center grew out of the E3 Group, which Professor I-Yun Lisa Hsieh 謝依芸 has led in the Department of Civil Engineering — with a joint appointment in Chemical Engineering — since 2020. What began as a research group focused on electric mobility grew across energy systems, economics, and environmental policy, and in 2025 was established as the NTU E3 Research Center.
