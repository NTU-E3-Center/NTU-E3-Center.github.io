About

Claire completed her undergraduate degree in Chemical Engineering with a minor in Bioenvironmental Systems Engineering at National Taiwan University (NTU) in 2025. She is currently pursuing a master’s degree at NTU’s Department of Chemical Engineering under the guidance of Professor Lisa Hsieh. Her research focuses on hydrogen energy and life cycle assessment (LCA). In 2025, she completed a CAE internship on the life cycle assessment of maritime alternative fuels, including hydrogen and ammonia.
