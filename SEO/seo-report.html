5GSC vs Google Analytics — what each tells you

You asked how these tools help your exposure. They answer different questions and you need both: Search Console is about getting found (before the click); Analytics is about what people do (after the click). Neither replaces the other.

🔎 Google Search Console (GSC)

Google's own view of how you appear in search results. The source of every number in this report. Free, authoritative for SEO.

Answers:
  • Which queries show your site, at what position & CTR
  • Which pages are indexed vs “discovered but not indexed”
  • Backlinks Google sees (Links report)
  • Rich-result / structured-data status & crawl errors
  • Sitemap fetch health
Blind to: what happens after the click — bounce, time, paths, conversions; and any non-Google traffic.
📊 Google Analytics 4 (GA4)

Behaviour on your site, across all sources (search, direct, referral, social). This is the G-… tag in your <head>.

Answers:
  • How visitors arrive (channels: organic, direct, referral, social)
  • Which landing pages get engagement vs bounce
  • Events: clicks on email/PDF, scroll depth, outbound links
  • Geography, device, returning vs new, session paths
  • Referral traffic from backlinks (the human side of §3-13)
Blind to: search queries, positions, impressions, indexing — everything pre-click.

How to use them together for exposure

1 · Find the leak

GSC says “high impressions, low CTR” → snippet problem (e.g. caece.net). GSC says “high CTR, low impressions” → ranking/indexing problem → build/optimise the page.

2 · Close the loop

GA4 says a page gets clicks but visitors bounce in 5s → the page doesn't deliver what the snippet promised. Fix the content, not the SEO.

3 · Prove value

Link GA4 ↔ GSC (Admin → Product links) so you can see, in one report, which queries lead to engaged sessions or a contact-form submit — the actual goal.

Set up these GA4 events (they're your real “conversions” for a lab): clicks on the contact email, /join & /collaborate CTA clicks, publication-PDF downloads, and outbound clicks to DOIs / member scholar profiles. Without events, GA4 only shows pageviews.

Step 1 — Keep G-JCJPED8JS6 as the canonical GA4

Step 2 — Check you're not double-counting

base.html loads GA4 G-JCJPED8JS6 twice over potentially: once as the hard-coded gtag.js block, and again if the GTM-M3L6SMNZ container also contains a GA4 tag for the same ID. That double-fires pageviews and inflates your numbers. Pick one delivery path: either keep the hard-coded gtag.js and make sure GTM has no GA4 tag, or move GA4 into GTM and remove the hard-coded block from base.html. Use GA4 DebugView or the Tag Assistant to confirm each pageview is counted once.

Consent mode (the EEA note): only relevant if you have meaningful European traffic. Yours is ~90% Taiwan + a small APAC/US tail, so this is low priority — but if you ever run ads or grow EU traffic, configure GTM consent mode then.
