This project develops a cross-national assessment of **green finance instruments**
— green bonds, sustainability-linked loans, blended-finance vehicles, and public
guarantee schemes — and evaluates how each performs under the contrasting market
structures and policy regimes of **Taiwan** and **India**.

Working with partners in both countries, the study builds a comparable evidence
base on instrument design, uptake, and effectiveness, and identifies the
conditions under which green finance most effectively mobilises private capital
toward net-zero transitions.

The two-year programme (2026–2027) is funded by Taiwan's National Science and
Technology Council under its international collaboration scheme.
