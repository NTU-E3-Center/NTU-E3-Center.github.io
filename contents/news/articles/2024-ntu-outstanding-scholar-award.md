We are proud to share that **[Associate Professor I-Yun Lisa Hsieh](/members/iyunlisahsieh/)** has been named an **NTU Outstanding Scholar (臺大拔萃學者)** for August 2024 through July 2027, awarded as the **Tsung Cho-Chang Outstanding Scholar (宗倬章先生拔萃學者)** — and, as the university's official records note, this is her **second time** receiving the distinction (第2次獲獎).

### Back-to-Back Recognition

Prof. Hsieh was first selected in the 2020 academic year as an **NTU Chen-Yung Outstanding Scholar (臺大勇源拔萃學者)**, holding the award from August 2020 to July 2023. The new term makes the recognition essentially continuous across her early career at NTU — a sustained institutional endorsement of the E3 group's research at the intersection of energy systems, transportation, and environmental policy. She is one of only a handful of two-time recipients on the university's all-time list, and one of just two Tsung Cho-Chang Outstanding Scholars selected in the 2024 cohort.

### About the Program

National Taiwan University established the program in 2019 to elevate its academic standing, and reorganized it in 2024 into two tracks: **Chair Professorships (拔萃講座)** for the most distinguished senior scholars, capped at six per academic year, and **Outstanding Scholars (拔萃學者)**, capped at twelve per year, each awarded for up to three years. The awards are endowed by philanthropic partners of the university — including the Fubon, Chen-Yung, and Tsung Cho-Chang educational foundations — linking private support to the university's mission of nurturing research excellence.

The honor arrived in a banner year for Prof. Hsieh, alongside her promotion to Associate Professor and the [Outstanding Young Transportation Professional Award](/news/2024-cit-young-transportation-award/) from the Chinese Institute of Transportation.
