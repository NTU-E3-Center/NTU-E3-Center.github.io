Congratulations to **[Yu-Shin Hu (胡羽忻)](/members/yushinhu/)** for winning the **Best Innovation Paper Award** at **iCHE 2024**!

### Research That Teaches Buildings and Vehicles to Cooperate

At the E3 Center, Yu-Shin's research asks how buildings and electric vehicles can manage energy together. Her master's work in the Department of Civil Engineering developed an adaptive, two-stage **vehicle-to-building (V2B) energy-management framework**: the first stage learns the diverse charging expectations of real users, while the second schedules charging and discharging under the uncertainty of vehicle availability and building demand. The result is a building that gains energy flexibility without asking drivers to sacrifice convenience — precisely the kind of methodological originality the Best Innovation Paper distinction is meant to recognize.

### The First of Several Recognitions

The iCHE 2024 award proved to be an early milestone in a strong run. The same research line later earned Yu-Shin the [Outstanding Master's Thesis Award from the Chinese Association for Energy Economics](/news/2025-caee-outstanding-thesis/), and she has first-authored publications with the lab on [AI-driven short-term load forecasting for university buildings](/publications/2025-ai-load-forecasting-university-buildings/) and [probabilistic electricity-demand forecasting](/publications/2026-probabilistic-building-load-forecasting/) — work that supplies the demand-side foresight her V2B scheduling framework depends on.

Seeing a master's student carry one research thread from a conference innovation award through to a national thesis prize and journal publications is exactly what the E3 Center hopes for from its students. We are incredibly proud of Yu-Shin's achievement!
