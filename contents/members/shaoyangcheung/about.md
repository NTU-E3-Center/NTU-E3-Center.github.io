Shao Yang Cheung earned his bachelor's degree in Civil Engineering from **National Taiwan University (NTU)**. He is currently pursuing a master's degree in the **Computer-Aided Engineering (CAE) division** of NTU's Department of Civil Engineering. His research focuses on **sustainable aviation fuel (SAF)**, particularly its carbon-reduction potential, economic feasibility, and practical limitations compared with conventional aviation fuel. Using **life cycle assessment (LCA)** and **techno-economic analysis (TEA)**, he evaluates the environmental and economic performance of the **Power-to-Liquid (PtL) pathway** for SAF production. His work aims to provide scientific evidence that supports informed fuel choices and the aviation industry's transition toward a low-carbon future.
