Kelechi Ukaoma earned her undergraduate degree in Operations Research and Financial Engineering from Princeton University in 2025, with a minor in Statistics & Machine Learning. She is currently a master's student in the Computer-Aided Engineering Division of the Department of Civil Engineering at National Taiwan University (NTU). Her current research interest centers around applications of network science in optimizing sustainable supply chains, especially in the face of high volatility. Her other interests include music composition, discussing New Urbanist movements, and learning languages.
