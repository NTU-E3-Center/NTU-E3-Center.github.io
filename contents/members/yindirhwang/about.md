Yin-Dir (YD) Hwang (黃尹笛) is a consulting advisor to the E3 Center, partnering with the team on agentic-AI product strategy and operations for energy-transition applications.

He is Senior Director of Agentic AI at Appier, where since 2017 he has built and led products spanning fraud detection, SaaS platforms, and large-scale agentic systems. He also serves as an adjunct lecturer at National Defense University, teaching AI trends and programming.

YD holds B.S. and M.S. degrees in Psychology from National Taiwan University. His graduate research on how fairness perception and emotion modulate decision making was published in Psychophysiology (2017).
